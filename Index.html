About Spice

Our Story

Founded in 2015, Spice was created to share the warmth and authenticity of Indian flavors with every guest. Our menu celebrates the rich culinary traditions of India, offering dishes that balance timeless recipes with fresh, homely preparation.

We specialize in traditional Indian cuisine, from aromatic curries and sizzling tandoori delights to festive sweets that bring comfort and joy. Every plate is crafted with care, ensuring that each bite carries the essence of India’s culinary heritage.

At Spice, food is more than taste—it’s an experience of culture, tradition, and togetherness.
